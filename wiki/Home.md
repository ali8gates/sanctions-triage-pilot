# Sanctions Triage Pilot

This wiki walks through a real pilot I built and shipped as an individual contributor: automating the front end review step of Varo's sanctions and PEP alert queue, in 90 days, with Claude Code.

It sits inside the same broader agentic AI program that was proposed for Varo across fraud, disputes, and financial crime. My piece was the sanctions and PEP alert review slice.

All figures on these pages that describe volumes, false positive rates, and staffing come straight from the discovery notes I wrote up while scoping this. No real customer data, watchlist record, or stakeholder name appears anywhere in this repo or on these pages. People are described by role, not by name.
